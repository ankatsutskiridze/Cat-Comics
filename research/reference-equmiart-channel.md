## The genre in rules

- **One premise = the night with the cat.** ~13 of the 15 top reels are bed / 3AM / waking
  the human. The rest are still home routine (litter box, feeding). Lowest performers are
  promo or trend posts with no human-and-cat moment.
- **Short.** Median of the top 15 ≈ 12 s; 4 of them under 9 s. One idea, no subplot.
- **The caption IS the premise**, written as a meme formula: "POV:", "Cats be like:",
  "Me: … / Also me:", "Mode: … ON", "It's 3am and…", a clock (1AM/2AM/3AM, 2:59 → 3:00).
  On screen from frame 0, top third, held all clip.
- **Mostly one shot, often a loop.** Many reels have 0-3 cuts (measured); the comedy is a
  looping action inside one framing (cat stomps, digs, zooms, sings), so the end runs into
  the start and the reel rewatches. Escalation by cutting to an extreme close-up (giant
  paws, side-eye face, screaming face) and back.
- **Musical.** A song or sound runs under the whole clip and the action is choreographed
  to it: the cat lip-syncs or "sings" (mic, disco lights), stomps / digs / bounces on the
  rhythm, a shouted lyric is typed on screen ("BREAKFAAAST TIMEEE"). The sound is half the
  joke. (Our sound rule is in CLAUDE.md "Sound": no copyrighted song in a file we upload.)
- **The cat is deadpan.** Half-lidded, unimpressed eyes as the resting face; the joke is
  the gap between the cat's calm and the chaos (or the human's wide sleepless eyes). Big
  emotion happens only in the close-up.
- **The human is a passive victim**: in bed, eyes wide open, tired, under the blanket. She
  barely moves; the cat drives every beat.

## Look

- Flat colour, thick dark outline, no gradients; a few motion lines and dust puffs.
- One limited palette per clip: night lavender / violet / mauve (most), a warm peach top
  view, a beige/grey day room.
- Very limited animation: 2-4 drawings per action, held frames, a pose cycle on loop,
  camera push-ins on still art.
- Two eras of the same brand: older reels have a typewriter-font caption in a black box and
  an @ handle; newer ones a rounded white caption box and a logo wordmark at the bottom.

## What we must make OURS (not hers)

Her signatures that we do NOT take: a grey deadpan cat + brown-haired woman in a lavender
night bedroom, her caption boxes, her wordmark placement, her exact gags (3AM stomp, singing
cat with mic, 2:59 → 3:00, pogo litter box). We take the grammar: meme-formula caption from
frame 0, one relatable home moment, a short looping action, escalation by close-up,
action choreographed to the music, deadpan pet vs suffering human.
