## Beats (1 fps frames)

| t (s) | shot | what happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0-4 | wide, static: night bedroom, bed, nightstand, window with moon | setup caption box "Cats for absolutely no reason..." top centre; grey cat walks across the sleeping human |
| 4-7 | same wide | caption gone; cat climbs the bed head / window sill |
| 8-11 | same wide | cat comes back, settles beside the human's face, stares |
| 12-14 | push-in, medium then close | calm cat face next to the sleeping human, eyes slowly widen |
| 15-16 | extreme close-up | **PUNCHLINE**: the cat screams, paws on cheeks, mouth wide open (panic for no reason) |
| 17-21 | medium | the cat clings to the human's shoulder, the human wakes up wide-eyed, half dragged off the bed by the cat |

## What makes it work

- **A premise everyone with a cat recognises**, stated in 4 words in the caption, so the
  viewer knows what to wait for by second 2.
- **Something moves from frame 0** (the cat is already walking).
- **Escalation by CAMERA**: wide → medium → extreme close-up as the tension rises; the
  close-up IS the punchline.
- **Calm to sudden panic**: the joke is the contrast; the human's wide-eyed wake-up is the
  aftermath.
- **Limited animation**: pose-to-pose, held drawings, small movement; the comedy comes from
  timing and expressions, not from smooth motion.
- Ends right after the payoff (no extended outro).

## Look

- Flat colour, thick dark outlines, no gradients, almost no shading.
- Limited pastel palette per scene (here: lavender walls, violet floor, mauve blanket, tan
  wood, deep-blue night window).
- Round, simple characters with BIG expressive eyes; the face carries the emotion.
- The caption is a white rounded box with black sans text, shown only during the setup.
- A wordmark logo sits at the bottom centre for the whole clip.

## Sound

- One pop song under the whole clip (continuous music, no gaps). **We do not do this with a
  copyrighted song** (CLAUDE.md "Sound").

## What we change when we adapt this genre

Our own characters and their design, our own palette and line style, our own situations
and jokes, our own logo and caption style, and licensed or royalty-free sound. We keep the
grammar: a relatable premise in the caption, motion from frame 0, escalation by camera, a
close-up punchline, and the clip ends on the payoff.
