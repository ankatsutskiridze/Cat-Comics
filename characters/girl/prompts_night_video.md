Attach BOTH reference sheets to every new chat:
- the girl: `characters/girl/reference_sheet_anka.jpeg`
- the cat: `characters/cat/reference_sheet_anka.jpeg`

Paste this line at the end of EVERY prompt:

> reference sheet (all black and fluffy, tall pointed ears, big cream-yellow eyes with thin

## Chat 1: the bed (generate 1, then EDIT it for 2-5 in the same chat)

next to the bed (bottom-left), looking up at her with its mouth wide open, meowing loudly.

the top fifth of the image.
