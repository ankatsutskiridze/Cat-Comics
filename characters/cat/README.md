# The cat (APPROVED: sheet v4, Anka 2026-10-25)

- Design source: `reference_sheet_anka.jpeg` (Anka, 2026-10-25), shapes measured off its
  head close-up and "In action" figures: tall sharp ears continuing the head's sides with a
  flat dip between them; a wide head with a near-flat bottom and paint drips under the chin;
  long thin hair spikes on the cheeks (no separate whiskers); big cream half-moon eyes with a
  steep V lid and a slit pupil hanging from the lid with a gold sliver; tiny pink nose and
  wide pink mouth; round paws.
- v4 (Anka, 2026-10-25): thick fur = a fuller body (every body radius x1.45) with a plain
  black brush edge: NO hairs sticking out of the body and NO coloured lines anywhere on it.
  Tail as on the sheet: wide where it grows out of the rump, a slim middle, a long oval
  paddle at the tip, a gentle S (no hook); sitting from behind it curls along the floor.
  Faces 'silly' (thin arched eyes, big tongue past the chin) and 'sleepy' (long thin
  closed-eye lines) copied from the sheet's expressions.
- Changed on Anka's request: NORMAL cat proportions (the sheet's "In action" figures), not
  the stretched hero; NO coloured drawings on the face or the tail (no tag, green strokes,
  forehead heart, tail heart, face sketch lines).
- Rig: `lib/cat.py` (3D stick skeleton, any yaw, poses are data, faces in `FACES`).
  Sheet: `python tools/make_cat_sheet.py <version>` -> `cat_sheet_v<version>.png`.
