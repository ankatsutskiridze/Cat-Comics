# My cat's night shift 😾⏰ #usa

The black cat wakes the red-haired girl every half hour from a new spot (the floor at
1:07, the nightstand at 1:43, the headboard at 2:19, the window sill at 2:56, her chest at
3:33). She sits up and yells "WHAT DO YOU WANT?!", the cat answers "NOTHING.", walks off,
and at 3:34 meows once more from the door. 23 s, 1080x1920.

Sound: no music, our own synthesised effects (`sound/night_shift_sfx.wav`, see
`sound/README.md`), -14.3 LUFS integrated / -7.2 dBFS peak measured on this mp4.
Source: `scenes/night_shift.py` + `scenes/night_shift_audio.py`. Art: the girl's scenes are
generated images (`characters/girl/scenes/`), the cat is cut-out generated poses
(`characters/cat/images/cut/`). Premise adapted from a genre reference; our characters,
clock times, spots, caption and ending.
