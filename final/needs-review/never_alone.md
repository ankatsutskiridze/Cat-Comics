# Not ONE minute alone 🐈‍⬛🙃 #usa

Four moments, one cat. "Me: trying to work": she types, the black cat jumps onto the laptop
keyboard (asdfjkl;;;;;) and sits there staring at her; she sighs. "Me: working out": its head
bobs along with her crunches, then it lands on her belly ("OOF!") and loafs there, purring; she
can't get up. "Me: brushing my teeth": it jumps into the sink and meows up at her. "Me: wanting
ONE cuddle": she opens her arms, "TOBY! Cuddle time!"; the cat looks at her, turns its head
away and jumps up onto the shelf. Her arms sink: "Oh, NOW you need space?" 19.8 s, 1080x1920.

Both characters drawn in code every frame (`lib/girl3.py`, `lib/cat3.py`); no generated image
is in the video. The four rooms are drawn in code too. Sound: no music, our own synthesised
effects (`sound/never_alone_sfx.wav`), -14.0 LUFS integrated / -4.5 dBFS peak measured on this mp4. Source: `scenes/never_alone.py` +
`scenes/never_alone_audio.py`. Premise: the genre's "life with a clingy cat" montage
(reference reel `research/reference-videos/ref_DeVH6yysZRj.mp4`); our situations, characters
and the reversal at the end.
