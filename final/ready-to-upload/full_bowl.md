# The bottom is visible 😾🍽️ #usa

At 7 AM the black cat screams at its food bowl. She shuffles in half asleep and says "It's
FULL." The cat stares, looks down at the bowl, looks back up: "MEOW." From above: the bowl is
full, except one tiny spot where the bottom shows. She sighs and nudges the bowl with her foot,
and the food slides over the spot. The cat eats ONE bite and the spot is back. "MEOW." She gives up
("...FINE.") and pours in more food until the bowl overflows; the cat eats, purring, and she
finally smiles. 21.6 s, 1080x1920. The pour is shown in the wide shot: she takes the bag off
the counter with her hand and tips it over the bowl.

First clip with both characters drawn in code every frame (`lib/girl3.py`, `lib/cat3.py`):
no generated image is in the video. Kitchen, bowl and food are drawn in code too.
Sound: no music, our own synthesised effects (`sound/full_bowl_sfx.wav`, see
`sound/README.md`), -14.0 LUFS integrated / -5.3 dBFS peak measured on this mp4.
Source: `scenes/full_bowl.py` + `scenes/full_bowl_audio.py`. Premise: the genre's "cat says
the full bowl is empty" moment; our characters, staging, close-up gag and loop ending.
