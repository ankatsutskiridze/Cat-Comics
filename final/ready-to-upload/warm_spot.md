# It wanted my pillow the whole time 😾🛏️ #usa

All night the black cat wakes her up, jumping from spot to spot while the bedside clock runs
from 1:12 to 3:38: it meows from the floor, sits on her chest and stares, meows from the window
sill, then screams "MEEEOOOW!!" right into her face from the pillow. She sits up: "WHAT DO YOU
WANT?!" It stares, hops into the warm dent her head left in the pillow, curls up and purrs.
"...my warm spot?!" 19.2 s, 1080x1920.

Both characters are drawn in code every frame (`lib/girl3.py`, `lib/cat3.py`); no generated
image is in the video. The bedroom is drawn in code too. The cat moves only by jumping
(crouch, arc, squash on landing): cat3 has no walk yet.
Sound: no music, our own synthesised effects (`sound/warm_spot_sfx.wav`, see
`sound/README.md`), -14.0 LUFS integrated / -5.5 dBFS peak measured on this mp4.
Source: `scenes/warm_spot.py` + `scenes/warm_spot_audio.py`. Premise: the genre's "the cat
wakes you all night" moment; our characters, perches, clock and our own ending (it wanted
the warm spot).
